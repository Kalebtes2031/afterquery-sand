The scoring and lives in BALLPOP are all over the place
What you see
Popping balls doesn't reliably pay out. The smallest ones in particular seem to be worth nothing at all — you can clear a screenful of them and the coin counter just sits there, so by the end of a run you're much poorer than the pops you actually landed should have made you. That makes the upgrades between waves feel out of reach.

Lives are just as unreliable. If a ball gets past the cannon, you lose a life and the total pool of lives quietly shrinks, so the run keeps getting harder in a way you never agreed to. From that point on, buying extra hearts stops helping — the capacity never grows again and you're stuck with the smaller pool for the rest of the run. On top of that, once you've collected a few hearts, the row of hearts in the top bar bursts out of its rounded bubble and the ones on the end get clipped off or vanish entirely, so you can't even tell how many you have left.
