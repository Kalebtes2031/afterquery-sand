The health and paint readouts stop making sense mid-run
What you see
After clearing a few drones while already topped up, the health readout stops behaving like a gauge: the coloured bar pushes past the outlined frame that's meant to contain it and smears across the corner of the screen. Grabbing refills when there's nothing left to refill keeps pumping the player's vitality up and up, well past what the game should ever allow, and the run turns into something that can't be lost.

The paint side is just as confused. Once a colour has run dry, that colour still fires happily, and its stock reads as something below empty rather than simply stopping. And right after picking up a drop while already fully stocked, there's a stretch where shooting is free — projectiles keep flying out while the stock gauge doesn't budge at all.

What correct looks like
The health readout should stay inside its frame, filling it completely at most and never spilling out. Picking up drops when you're already fully stocked should feel like a no-op — nothing about your health or paint changes.
