The mission HUD and weapon loadout are off
What you see
Once I pick a sortie and a weapons package at the F.O.B. and get into the fire-support mission, the readout down in the bottom-left corner blows up sideways across the whole bottom of the screen as one long run-on jumble of text. It stretches so far that it eats into the space where the sortie information is supposed to sit, so I can't read either one properly.

There's also a problem with the gun that carries two different kinds of shell. When the sortie starts, one of the two shell stocks is loaded up far lighter than the other, so that option runs dry very early and I'm stuck with only the one type for most of the mission.

What correct looks like
The bottom-left readout should stay a small, tidy box tucked in the corner with a short, readable weapon label, leaving the readout on the other side of the screen clear and legible. And the gun that carries two shell types should start the sortie with even stocks of both, so switching between them is a real tactical choice rather than one being gone almost immediately.
