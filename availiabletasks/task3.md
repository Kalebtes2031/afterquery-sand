The matrix chart is hard to read
What you see
The matrix shows its grid and the plotted items just fine, but there's no way to tell what any position actually means. Along the bottom edge of the white plot card, where the scale numbers should run, there's nothing at all — the labels seem to be pushed outside the card and get cut off. The same thing happens down the left side: the vertical scale is simply missing, clipped away somewhere beyond the edge of the card.

The result is a chart you can look at but not read. You can see roughly where things sit relative to each other, but you can't say what value any item has on either axis, so comparing or reporting on anything plotted here is guesswork.

What correct looks like
Both scales should be plainly visible inside the plot card — numbers running along the bottom edge and numbers running down the left edge, lined up with the grid so that each plotted item can be read off against them at a glance.
