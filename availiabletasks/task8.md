The level progress display is out of sync
What you see
Once you get past the very first level, the LEVEL badge in the corner stops telling the truth — the number it shows doesn't match the level you're actually flying through. Levels that should feel like deep, late-game runs behave as if you were still near the beginning of the journey, with easier setups than they ought to have. When you finally touch the flag, the saved BEST that gets recorded is behind the level you just cleared, and the "next level incoming" message names a level that isn't the one you're about to play.

On top of that, the BEST badge is pushed off the left edge of the screen, so part of it is chopped off and it no longer sits neatly beneath the LEVEL badge like it should.

What correct looks like
The corner badges should always reflect reality: the number you see is the level you're playing, later levels should feel appropriately deep into the journey, and clearing a level should record it as your best and correctly announce whatever comes next. Both badges should be fully visible and tidily stacked in the corner.
