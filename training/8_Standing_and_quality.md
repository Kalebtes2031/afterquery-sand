# Standing and Quality

## 🛡️ Ramp-Up & Initial Review Limits

During your first **3 completed tasks**, the platform limits the volume of concurrent work you can have awaiting review. 

* **Purpose:** Ensures misunderstandings are caught early before submitting large batches.
* **Review Path:** Every submission goes through both automated QA and expert human review.

---

## 📈 Maintaining Full Standing

* **Full Access:** Consistent clean review outcomes graduate you to full, unrestricted task claiming.
* **Access Risk:** Repeated low-quality submissions or rejected fix cycles can pause or permanently revoke claiming access.
* **Continuous Controls:** Automated quality controls remain active throughout your tenure.

---

## 💡 Best Practice: When in Doubt, Communicate or Release

> [!TIP]
> **Handoff Over Guessing:** If you are unsure whether an issue constitutes a valid defect, communicate clearly or release the task. A careful, early handoff is far cheaper than a false submission that burns your retry budget.
